Handwritten math recognition model
==================================

encoder.onnx and decoder.onnx are the CoMER model (Zhao and Gao, "CoMER:
Modeling Coverage for Transformer-based Handwritten Mathematical
Expression Recognition", ECCV 2022, https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.04410),
from the checkpoint published at https://github.com/Green-Wood/CoMER
(lightning_logs/version_0). tool/math_model/ re-implements the network,
loads those weights (tensors only), exports it to ONNX as an encoder and a
cached step decoder, and stores the weights as 8-bit integers.

The checkpoint was trained on the CROHME 2014 data set, which is
distributed for research under Creative Commons BY-NC-SA 3.0. The CoMER
repository itself carries no license. Treat this model as suitable for
personal, non-commercial use: before Endless is sold or otherwise used
commercially, replace it with a model trained on data whose license allows
that (tool/math_model/README.md says how the app expects a model).
