Speech to text and speakers (Phase 8a)
======================================

Everything here runs on the device. Nothing is sent anywhere.

  https://github.com/openai/whisper. Converted to the ggml format and
  quantized to 5 bits by the whisper.cpp project (Georgi Gerganov and

  5359861c739e955e79d9a303bcbc70fb988958b1 (SHA-256 422f1ae4…a8898).
  Run by whisper.cpp 1.9.5 (MIT License), built from source in
  packages/endless_speech.

speakers-ecapa.onnx
  The ECAPA-TDNN speaker embedding from SpeechBrain
  (speechbrain/spkrec-ecapa-voxceleb, revision
  0f99f2d0ebe89ac095bcc5903c4dd8f72b367286), Apache License 2.0,
  https://speechbrain.github.io. Trained on VoxCeleb 1 and 2 (Creative
  Commons Attribution 4.0). Exported to ONNX, with its filterbank features

  integers, by tool/speech_model/export_speakers.py.
